Why do we wait for the right moment to start changing our lives?

We know something in life needs to change, but we keep putting it off. Why the perfect moment almost never comes, and how to start without waiting for perfect conditions.

October 29, 2026 3-minute read By Alon Shemesh

This article is written for women and men alike.

How many times have you told yourself "Once this stressful period is over, I'll start investing in myself," "When I feel more confident, I'll take the step" or "Maybe next year will be the right time"?

We know something in our lives needs to change, but we keep putting it off. Sometimes it's the wish to advance in our career, build self-confidence, step out of our comfort zone or simply feel that we're living in line with what we truly want.

The problem is that the perfect moment almost never comes. There will always be commitments, worries and good reasons to wait. And so, without noticing, the wish to change becomes just another thought that stays in our head.

Why do we put off the very things that matter to us?

Putting off change isn't necessarily a sign of low motivation. Sometimes, the more meaningful the goal, the greater the fear of starting.

Change requires us to face uncertainty, give up familiar habits and risk the possibility that we won't succeed the way we hoped.

Some people wait for complete certainty before they act, and others believe they first need to improve themselves in order to deserve success.

But personal empowerment doesn't begin the moment fear disappears; it begins when we learn to act even while it's there.

How does waiting for the right moment keep us in place?

When we keep putting off what we want, a feeling of being stuck can develop.

We see others moving forward, compare ourselves to them and feel that time is passing without us doing anything meaningful for ourselves.

Over time, putting things off can also hurt our sense of capability. Every time we promise ourselves to start and don't, the thought that maybe we're not capable of changing grows stronger.

This is exactly where a process of empowerment and personal growth begins: in identifying the thoughts and habits that hold us back, and in understanding that we can choose a different path.

How do you start changing without waiting for perfect conditions?

The first step doesn't have to be dramatic. Sometimes meaningful change begins with a small decision you can put into practice today.

Instead of setting a general goal like "I want a better life," ask yourself what exactly you would like to change, and what is the smallest step that could bring you closer to it.

It's also important to tell the difference between real obstacles and the fears and assumptions that keep us from moving forward.

Personal development coaching can help identify thinking patterns that hold you back, strengthen your sense of capability and build practical steps toward change. Through personal empowerment and personal growth, you can work on your wishes, strengths and personal blocks, and develop a path that fits your pace and your goals.

You don't have to be completely ready to start

One of the most important things in a process of change is understanding that you don't need to know in advance how everything will work out.

Sometimes the confidence we're waiting for develops through experience, not before it.
