My story

My path to coaching went through life itself

I didn't come to this world from books. I came to it because I, too, knew periods of feeling stuck up close, of inner noise, of feeling that life was happening to me instead of me leading it. The moment I realized it could be different changed my life, and led me to learn how to help others reach that same moment themselves.

I studied personal coaching at the Tut Tikshoret school, earned my NLP Master certification and went deeper into tools for emotional work. Since then I've guided people of all ages - teens, discharged soldiers starting out in life, couples at challenging crossroads, and adults who decided it was time to live differently.

What I believe

Real change doesn't happen in a lecture - it happens in a process

I don't believe in motivational talks that lift you up for two days and then fade. I believe in a structured, personal and practical process that connects three things:

  • Mind - recognizing the patterns and beliefs that hold you back, and changing them at the root with NLP.
  • Emotion - making room for what you truly feel, regulating the inner noise and building confidence and calm.
  • Action - translating it all into daily habits and measurable steps, so the change lasts.

Every process with me is built from scratch - according to your personality, your reality and your pace. There's no one template that fits everyone, so everything starts with a simple, human intro call.

My tools

How do we reach the subconscious?

Most of the techniques in the process work on the subconscious - because that's where, and not in the conscious mind, the patterns, beliefs and emotions that run us behind the scenes reside.

You can compare it to an iceberg: what you see from the outside is only the tip - the behavior, the words, what's easy to talk about. Below the surface lies a whole depth, layer upon layer of experiences, emotions and beliefs that don't always have words. Many people find it hard to simply "open up and talk" - and that's exactly why there are tools that know how to reach inside, gently and safely.

🧘

Guided imagery - the central tool in the NLP method

One-on-one or in a group: calm background music, deep relaxation of the body and breath, and a guided story built just for you. As you connect to the characters and situations in the story, the emotions get room, what weighs on you is released - and from there a personally tailored solution can be built, for teens and adults, for women and men.

  • Deep relaxation that reduces stress and anxiety - in body and mind
  • Building an inner "safe place" of control and stability that you can return to at any moment
  • Gentle processing of trauma, fears and difficult emotions
  • Also suitable for self-practice at home - the tool stays with you
🎴

Inspiration cards - a bridge between the mind and the heart

Cards that combine a visual image with a word or sentence - not fortune-telling cards, but a therapeutic tool that evokes personal associations and bypasses rational barriers. When it's hard to find words, when you're closed off or introverted - the card offers an opening to share what's on your heart, and the barriers open at your own pace.

  • Excellent conversation starters - even for those who find it hard to share
  • Uncovering emotions and beliefs from the subconscious in a safe, accessible way
  • Developing self-awareness, creativity and mental flexibility
  • An "accelerator" for breakthroughs in processes that got stuck
