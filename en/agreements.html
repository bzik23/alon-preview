Consent Statement and Service Agreement for Coaching and NLP

For adults - a personal coaching and NLP process.

Download the document (PDF)

This document constitutes a legal and binding service agreement and an informed consent statement between the Therapist and the service recipient (hereinafter: the "Coachee"). The purpose of this document is to set out the limits of professional responsibility, define the nature of the service and ensure fully aligned expectations under standards of ethics and legal compliance.

Definition of the Service and the Therapist's Training

The service provided in this framework is based on personal coaching methodologies and the use of NLP (Neuro-Linguistic Programming) tools. It is hereby expressly clarified that the Therapist is an NLP Master and a personal coach only. The Therapist is not a licensed psychologist, social worker or clinical/didactic diagnostician. The process focuses on identifying emotional patterns, changing habits, developing resilience and improving quality of life through work with the conscious mind and the subconscious.

Legal and Diagnostic Validity and Medical Limitations

  1. The coaching process, its accompanying documentation and the results arising from it are intended for purposes of personal development and growth only.
  2. The content of the sessions and the professional summaries do not constitute an expert opinion for legal purposes, are not admissible as evidence in courts of law and do not constitute a clinical or medical diagnosis.
  3. The process is not a substitute for medical or psychiatric treatment. The Therapist is not qualified to give advice on medication, and the Coachee undertakes not to make changes to any existing medication treatment without consulting a licensed physician.

Confidentiality and Professional Ethics

The Therapist is committed to maintaining professional confidentiality and the Coachee's privacy. This commitment is intended to allow full therapeutic presence and emotional containment based on trust and security. Any information provided during the sessions will be kept confidential, except in the following cases in which there is a duty to report under law:

  • A real and concrete concern of self-harm, or the emergence of suicidal intentions.
  • A concern or declared intention to harm another person.
  • Information about planning to cause damage to property or to commit illegal acts.

Client's Declaration and Waiver of Claims

I, the undersigned, declare that I have received a detailed explanation of the working methods used in the process, which include, among others: guided imagery, meditation, NLP techniques, the use of inspiration cards, work with symbols, tools from the field of psychosomatics (the mind-body connection) and techniques for regulating the nervous system. I understand that this is a personal process whose results vary and depend on the degree of my personal involvement and commitment.

I hereby declare that I waive any future allegation, demand or claim for damage, emotional distress or failure to achieve results as a consequence of the process. Sole responsibility for applying the tools and for making personal decisions during the process and after it rests with me alone.

Definition of the Service and the Therapist's Training

The work process for the minor is based on the principles of NLP and emotional coaching. It is clarified that the Therapist is an NLP Master and a personal coach. The Therapist is not a child psychologist, social worker or didactic/clinical diagnostician. The process is intended to help with identifying emotions, building self-confidence and improving nervous-system and emotional regulation skills, using experiential tools such as guided imagery, meditation and work with symbols.

Legal Validity and Medical Limitations

The process and its documentation are intended solely to promote the emotional well-being of the minor. The content of the sessions shall not be regarded as a medical diagnosis, a psychiatric assessment or a legal opinion, and is not intended for presentation in court as expert testimony. The process is not a substitute for psychiatric treatment or advice on medication.

Confidentiality, Ethics and the Duty to Report

We recognize the minor's right to privacy as a critical element in creating therapeutic presence and containment. However, the duty of confidentiality is subject to ethical limitations and to the duty to report to the parent/guardian and to the authorities in the following cases:

  1. Risk to self, self-harm or suicidal thoughts on the part of the minor.
  2. A real concern that the minor will harm another person.
  3. A report of an intention to carry out illegal activity or damage to property.

Guardian's Declaration and Waiver of Claims

I, as the parent/legal guardian of the minor, approve their participation in the process. I have received an explanation of the tools that will be used (guided imagery, work with symbols, inspiration cards, nervous-system regulation and the like). I understand that the results are individual and depend on the minor's cooperation.

On behalf of the minor and on my own behalf, I waive any future claim or allegation against the Therapist for direct or indirect damages arising from the coaching process or from its emotional consequences.
